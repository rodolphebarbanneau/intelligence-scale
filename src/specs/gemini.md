# Gemini

## Product

Gemini is Google’s AI assistant. Google documents the product as Gemini Apps: a signed-in (and, on the web, sometimes signed-out) chat product at gemini.google.com, plus the Gemini mobile app, Gemini in Chrome, Gemini in Google Messages, Gemini Go on Android Go devices, and Gemini on some headphones and smartwatches. People type or talk, attach files, switch models, and ask Gemini to use Connected Apps. The product is the Gemini Apps chassis, not the Gemini models sold on their own.

Personal accounts sign in with a Google Account they manage. Work accounts need a qualifying Google Workspace edition and must be 18 or over. School accounts need the service enabled by the institution’s administrator. Family Link managers can turn Gemini Apps off for users under 13 (or the applicable age in their country). Family Link–managed accounts cannot use the Gemini web app. gemini.google.com supports Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera, or Edgium.

Google One sells Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra for personal accounts. Those plans raise compute-based usage limits and unlock some features. Work and school access follows Workspace licenses: additional service, core service, AI Expanded Access, or AI Ultra Access, with different model limits and data-handling terms. Feature availability also varies by country, language, age, device, and whether Keep Activity is on.

This file covers Gemini Apps. It does not cover Vertex AI or the Gemini API on Google Cloud, Google Flow, Jules, Google Antigravity, Project Genie, or Gemini side panels inside Gmail, Docs, Vids, and other Workspace apps, except where Gemini Apps Help describes connecting those services from Gemini Apps. Gemini Notebook is a separate notebook product that can connect to and sync notebooks with Gemini Apps.

## Features

### Gemini Apps

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en

- People open gemini.google.com, enter a prompt, optionally add files or images, and submit. Gemini Apps can brainstorm, summarize, and draft text such as outlines, emails, and blog posts.
- Signed-in users can start a temporary chat that does not appear in Gemini Apps Activity or recent chats. Temporary chats are for personal accounts on the web or mobile app, not Gemini in Chrome or Gemini in Google Messages, and they cannot use Gems, Connected Apps that need Keep Activity, or personalization.
- Signed-in users switch models from the model name in the text box. Documented models are Gemini Flash-Lite, Gemini Flash, and Gemini Pro.
- Users can edit a prompt and regenerate the response, view other drafts for some prompts that did not use apps, copy code blocks, and ask for a chart from data in a response.
- Gemini Apps can generate formatted files from a chat as Workspace Docs or Sheets, PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, LaTeX, TXT, RTF, or Markdown, then download them or export them to Google Drive.
- Some devices can use text chat without signing in. Signed-out use does not include past chats, personalization, Connected Apps, image generation, Gems, or file upload.
- Gemini Apps may use general location, or precise location if allowed. The web app shows whether precise location is in use in Settings & help.
- Supported browsers for gemini.google.com are Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera, or Edgium. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668?hl=en
- Sign-in accepts a personal Google Account, a work account on a qualifying Workspace edition (18+), or a school account enabled by an administrator. Family Link–managed accounts cannot access the Gemini web app. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668?hl=en
- Users can import remembered facts from another AI platform by pasting an export prompt response, and can import chat history from ChatGPT or Claude as a ZIP up to 5 GB (up to five ZIPs per day). Import is for personal accounts 18 or older and is not available in the EEA, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, or in Gemini in Google Messages, Gemini in Chrome, or Gemini on Android XR. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16868299?hl=en
- Deep Think is an experimental Pro thinking level for Google AI Ultra or Google AI Ultra for Business users 18 or older. Responses can take a few minutes; the web app marks the thread when they are ready. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16345172?hl=en
- Where available, thinking level can be Standard, Extended, or Deep Think (Ultra only, Pro model). Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en

### Gemini mobile app

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984?hl=en

- On iPhone and iPad, the Gemini app supports typed chat, voice prompts, Live, image creation, and camera input. Some features work without signing in; saving activity requires a personal, work, or school account with Gemini Apps access.
- A voice prompt keeps the microphone open for up to five minutes until the user stops, resumes, or sends.
- Gemini Go is a streamlined Gemini on Android Go devices with 2 GB of RAM or more. It is part of the Google Search app, is rolling out gradually, and can call or text contacts, find local info, set alarms or calendar events, upload files, and play music. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17079170?hl=en
- Work or school users who open the Gemini mobile app from an Android Work Profile are sent to the Gemini web app. Assistant-powered quick voice actions are unavailable, and those users cannot delete chats or create public chat links in the mobile app. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14620100?hl=en

### Gemini Live

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15274899?hl=en

- Gemini Live is a spoken, interruptible conversation on the Gemini mobile app or Gemini as the mobile assistant. The Live help article says it is not available in the Gemini web app or Gemini in Google Messages.
- Users can start Live from the app, by saying “Hey Google, let’s talk Live” or “Hey Google, let’s talk,” or on Pixel Buds.
- Live can stream the camera or share the full device screen. Camera sharing stops on hold, leaving the app, or lock; screen sharing stops on hold or lock and must be started again.
- Live can run in the background or continue on a locked phone when Gemini on lock screen and Live notifications are on. Users cannot start Live from a locked screen.
- Users can hold, mute, end, resume, switch between Live and text in the same chat, change Gemini’s voice, turn interrupt-on-speech on or off, and turn on captions.
- Some Connected Apps work in Live, including Google Workspace, Calendar, Keep, Tasks, Home, Device assistance, YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, Shopping, and several device note and calendar apps. Work or school Live chats currently support only Calendar, Keep, and Tasks, and the administrator must enable those apps.
- Guided Vision can give audio cues while the camera is on in Live, such as help reading a small label.
- Gemini in Chrome also has Live: users can talk while browsing, share the current tab, and ask Gemini to scroll to or highlight content on the page. Voice navigation of PDFs is not supported. Live in Chrome is not available to users under 18. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16363185?hl=en

### Create, learn & research

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111?hl=en

- Deep Research builds a research plan, then a report. Default source is Google Search; users can add Gmail or Drive when Google Workspace is connected, upload files, or add NotebookLM notebooks, and can deselect Search.
- Users 18 or older and signed in can run Deep Research. Pro and Ultra users can generate reports with Pro; all users can use Thinking. Limits cover daily requests and concurrent runs.
- Ultra reports can include charts, diagrams, and interactive simulators when Workspace services are not selected as sources.
- Reports can be opened later when Keep Activity is on, turned into an Audio Overview, visualized in Canvas, shared, exported to Docs, or copied.
- Nano Banana 2 generates and edits images from the Images sidebar or by prompt. Paid subscribers can redo an image with Nano Banana Pro when the Gemini model is Pro. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560?hl=en
- Image generation requires sign-in. Editing requires age 18 or over. Download resolution is 2K with a Google AI plan and 1K without. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560?hl=en
- Nano Banana 2 Lite is used with Flash-Lite and does not support multiple image references or multiple edits. Nano Banana 2 is used with Flash or Pro. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560?hl=en
- Users can record a face and voice avatar and reuse it in Nano Banana images, and can mention `@` plus their Google username to use the avatar in Omni videos. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560?hl=en
- Gemini Omni generates and edits video from text, images, or video for personal Google AI plan users or work/school users with a qualifying license, age 18 or over. Users can upload one video and up to five images, pick a template and aspect ratio, edit across turns, add audio, and download or share to YouTube. Uploading video for edits is unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and some US states. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339?hl=en
- Lyria generates music tracks from a prompt, optional images or videos, length (about 1 minute or 2–3 minutes), vocals, and genre. Full tracks require the Pro model. Tracks download as MP4 with cover art or MP3 and carry a digital watermark. Users must be 18 or older, signed in, and have Keep Activity on. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16901237?hl=en
- Canvas creates or edits a doc, app, slides, or code in a side panel. Users can edit code, view console logs, select-and-ask, add Gemini features to an app (18+), restyle docs, add LaTeX, and create a slideshow, Audio Overview, quiz, infographic, or web page from a doc (18+). Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047321?hl=en
- Canvas content can be shared with a `g.co/gemini/share` link, exported to Docs or Slides, copied, or (for Python) exported to Colab. Shared Canvas links open only on gemini.google.com. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047321?hl=en
- Audio Overviews turn documents, slides, NotebookLM notebooks, or Deep Research reports into a podcast-style conversation that can be played, downloaded, or shared. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047373?hl=en
- The Storybook Gem creates an illustrated storybook from a prompt or photos, can read it aloud with a chosen voice, and can be shared or printed. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16434396?hl=en

### Notebooks

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16972047?hl=en

- When Gemini Notebook is connected, notebooks are a dedicated chat that keeps sources, instructions, and discussion. Notebooks created or edited in Gemini Apps sync with Gemini Notebook.
- Notebooks are available on gemini.google.com and the Gemini mobile app, and can be used in the Gemini app on Mac even when the Gemini Notebook connected app is not available there. The feature is rolling out gradually. In the EEA, Gemini Notebook must be connected manually.
- With Keep Activity off, users can still chat in a notebook but cannot auto-save chats, use notebook memory, add existing chats from the sidebar, add Drive sources, or use study or business notebooks.
- Users can create, rename, pin, instruct, and delete notebooks, add chats to a notebook, and add sources: device files, Google Drive, URLs, or copied text. Plan limits go up to 600 sources. A notebook cannot use another notebook as a source.
- Study notebooks (Keep Activity on, web only, not Mac) create diagnostic quizzes, personalized lessons, and progress tracking, and can make flashcards and infographics from course materials.
- Connecting Google Business Profile enables a business notebook. Shared notebooks in Gemini Notebook are not listed in the Gemini Apps sidebar.

### Connected Apps

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044?hl=en

- Signed-in users connect apps so Gemini can complete requests and take actions in those apps. Users mention an app with `@` or let Gemini pick one. Availability depends on location, language, device, account type, and Gemini app.
- Connected Apps are not available in Gemini in Google Messages. With Keep Activity off, they are unavailable on the web, iOS, and watches; Android still offers Device assistance, Phone, Messages, and WhatsApp.
- Gemini always uses public Google Search. With Keep Activity on it can also use public Flights, Hotels, Maps, and YouTube information without accessing personal content unless the user permits it.
- Google Workspace can find, manage, and summarize content in Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Keep, Tasks, Chat, and Meet, and can create calendar events, Keep notes, and Tasks. It cannot access Docs or Gmail comments or images, Drive pictures and videos outside docs/spreadsheets/presentations/PDFs, or manage Drive folders. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15229592?hl=en
- The official availability table lists Connected Apps including Contacts, Messages, Phone, WhatsApp, Canva, Wix, Device assistance, Google Home, Zocdoc, Google Play, Instacart, OpenTable, Zillow, Angi, Thumbtack, Google Photos, iHeartRadio, Pandora, Samsung Gallery, Spotify, YouTube Music, Dropbox, GitHub, Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Google Classroom, Google Workspace, Granola, Otter.ai, Fever, GetYourGuide, Localiza, Ticketmaster, Viator, and manufacturer calendar/notes apps. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/table/17434654?hl=en
- Users can connect a custom app by adding a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server URL in the Gemini web app. Custom apps require age 18+, a US personal account, Keep Activity, English, and the web or mobile Gemini app. Google does not control, monitor, or secure third-party MCP servers; write actions require manual confirmation. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137?hl=en
- GitHub can import a repository into a web-app chat so the user can ask about its code (personal, work, or school; 18+). Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/table/17434654?hl=en

### Gemini Spark, schedules & skills

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507?hl=en

- Gemini Spark is an experimental personal agent that runs tasks from Connected Apps, skills, chats, signed-in websites, Personal Intelligence, location, a remote browser, and a remote computer. Google labels it experimental and in early development and says user supervision is required.
- Spark requires age 18+, a personal Google Account, Google AI Pro or Ultra, and Keep Activity. It is unavailable in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, and is offered on the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, and gemini.google.com.
- Users switch to Spark in the sidebar, describe a task, optionally set when it should run, attach `/` skills, files, Drive items, or a notebook, and watch a work panel. Gemini may ask the user to take over browser steps.
- Up to 15 tasks can run at once. Schedules do not start if 15 tasks are already running. Usage counts against Gemini Apps compute limits.
- Users can pause or resume schedules, delete local Chrome data, remote browser data, and remote code-execution data, turn Spark off (which deletes remote browser and code-execution data and pauses schedules), and turn it back on to resume paused work.
- Spark schedules are separate from scheduled actions in Gemini chats. Spark schedules can fire at a time or on an event such as an email arriving. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710?hl=en
- Skills are reusable instructions and context for Spark only. Users create them from a template or uploaded file. Spark can apply a skill automatically or when the user types `/`. Multiple skills can be combined. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094296?hl=en
- Scheduled actions in ordinary Gemini chats are recurring prompts (up to 10 active). Keep Activity must be on. Paid plans prepare the response in the hour before delivery; accounts without a Google AI plan may prepare it several hours earlier. Users manage them under Settings & help → Scheduled actions. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16316416?hl=en

### Gems

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780?hl=en

- Gems are saved custom instructions. Users create, edit, and delete custom Gems on the Gemini web app and use them on web and mobile. Gems cannot be used with Gemini Live.
- A Gem can include Knowledge files from the device, Google Drive (Keep Activity on and Workspace connected), or a NotebookLM notebook. Drive files stay current as the Drive file changes. Citations to knowledge files can be disabled.
- Gemini can rewrite Gem instructions from a short draft. Premade Gems include Storybook; Learning coach does not currently support language learning.
- A Gem created on the web also appears in the Gemini mobile app and the Gemini side panel in Google Workspace.
- Shared Gems are added under My Gems after the recipient opens and uses them. Experimental Gems expose early-stage features whose quality and availability may vary.

### Personalization

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598406?hl=en

- Personal Intelligence is a gradual opt-in for personal accounts 18 or older with Keep Activity on. It is not available in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, and not in Gems. Surfaces include the mobile app, gemini.google.com, Gemini in Chrome where available, and Gemini on a smartwatch.
- Connected apps for Personal Intelligence are Contacts, Google Photos (some countries), Google Wallet (US), Google Workspace, Search services, and YouTube. Users can connect or disconnect them and turn Personal Intelligence off for a single chat.
- Instructions for Gemini apply to every chat for personal accounts on the web app, mobile app, Gemini in Chrome, and Gemini on a smartwatch, and are not available for work, school, or supervised accounts or in Gems. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625?hl=en
- Memory uses past Gemini chats to personalize later replies and can be turned on or off. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en
- Daily Brief is a gradual, English-only, US personal-account feature (18+) that needs Personal Intelligence, connected Google Workspace, and Memory. It shows Top of mind items from Gmail, Calendar, and chats, and Looking ahead suggestions. Users can chat about, complete, dismiss, or rate items, or turn Daily Brief off. It appears on mobile, gemini.google.com, and Gemini Live. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17077455?hl=en

### Work or school Google Account

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14620100?hl=en

- Most work or school users can use Gemini Apps. Feature set and data handling follow the Workspace license. Work users must be 18 or over.
- Additional-service access uses Google Terms of Service and the Gemini Apps Privacy Notice and needs administrator enablement. Core-service licenses use Google Workspace Terms and, for listed editions, enterprise-grade data protections: chats and uploads are not reviewed by humans or used to improve generative AI models.
- License rows in Help include standard, Pro, AI Expanded Access, and AI Ultra Access, with different prompt caps, context-window sizes, and feature caps for Audio Overviews, Deep Research, Deep Think 3.1, Nano Banana, scheduled actions, music, video, and slides.
- Administrators can add Google AI Ultra for Business. Users see a Pro, Expanded, or Ultra badge on gemini.google.com when those licenses apply.
- For work or school, Keep Activity is on by default and only the administrator can turn it off. History defaults to 18 months; the admin can set 3, 18, or 36 months or turn history off.
- Gemini in Chrome is pending HIPAA, SOC, ISO, and other compliance certifications described in Google’s turn-on/off documentation.
- There are separate usage limits for Gemini inside Workspace apps; those limits are not the Gemini Apps limits.

### Plans, limits & upgrades

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en

- Usage is compute-based: prompt complexity, models and features, and chat length. The limit refreshes every five hours until a weekly cap. Users see remaining capacity under Settings → Usage Limits.
- Personal plan multipliers versus standard limits: AI Plus 2×, AI Pro 4×, AI Ultra 5× or 20× versus Pro depending on the subscription. Context windows: 32k without a plan, 128k on Plus, 1 million on Pro and Ultra.
- After a paid-plan limit, chats can continue on Flash-Lite. Limits can change with demand; Deep Research may be unavailable for users without a Google AI plan during high demand.
- Help lists feature rows that depend on plan, including Canvas, Gems, Storybook, Connected Apps, quizzes and flashcards, Audio Overviews, screen automation, slide generation, Deep Research, Daily Brief, Gemini Spark, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro redo, video, music, scheduled actions, and priority access to some new features.
- Personal Google AI upgrades are sold through Google One. Users 18 or older manage Plus, Pro, and Ultra from Gemini Apps or Google One and can change or cancel in Google One. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446?hl=en
- The Gemini about page lists Free plus Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra, with higher Gemini app limits, Flow credits, and other Google One bundle items. Cancel anytime; no refunds for partial periods except where law requires. Source: https://gemini.google/about/
- File uploads: up to 10 supported files per prompt; video up to 2 GB and 5 minutes (1 hour on Pro or Ultra); other files up to 100 MB; audio up to 10 minutes (3 hours on Pro or Ultra); one code folder or GitHub repo up to 5,000 files and 100 MB. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178?hl=en

### Gemini in other Google products

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16283624?hl=en

- Gemini in Chrome is a gradual Chrome side panel on Chromebook Plus, Mac, or Windows for signed-in Chrome users 13 or older in supported regions. It is unavailable in Incognito. Work or school access (18+) needs administrator enablement.
- Chrome shares the current tab by default and can share up to 10 open tabs. Users can `@` a tab, pop out or dock the chat, switch models, go Live, and ask Gemini to summarize, compare pages, draft Gmail messages, look up Photos, or complete multi-step actions.
- Chrome settings include precise location, microphone, share current tab by default, and “Let Gemini browse for you” (auto browse). Users under 18 cannot use microphone, closed captions, or auto browse.
- Sharing a Workspace URL can let Gemini read the full Workspace file, not only the visible tab. Connecting the Workspace app is required for broader Workspace content.
- Gemini in Google Messages is an RCS chat with Gemini on Android for personal accounts 18 or older (not Family Link or Workspace). Chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Users can attach a photo and can turn the Gemini button off in Messages settings. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14599070?hl=en

### Privacy, activity & security

Primary source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en

- The Gemini Apps Privacy Hub states how Gemini Apps collect prompts, uploads, Connected Apps data (including MCP tools), location, and Live audio, video, and screenshares, and how users manage those settings.
- With Keep Activity on, activity is stored in the Google Account and, for personal accounts, may be used to improve Google services, including training generative AI models, and may be reviewed by humans. Users can review and delete activity at myactivity.google.com/product/gemini. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en
- Keep Activity defaults on for users 18 or over. Turning it off still stores conversations up to 72 hours for service and feedback; that activity does not appear in Gemini Apps Activity. Gems remain saved; Gem chats are not saved. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en
- Auto-delete defaults to 18 months and can be 3 months, 36 months, or off. An optional setting (off by default) lets Google use audio and Live video/screenshares to improve services. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en
- Users manage Gems, skills, public links, Connected Apps (including MCP), saved info, Spark settings, Memory, and Personal Intelligence from Gemini settings and the Privacy Hub.
- Gemini Apps may refuse or remove generated images, videos, or Gems when systems detect a possible Terms of Service or Prohibited Use Policy violation.
